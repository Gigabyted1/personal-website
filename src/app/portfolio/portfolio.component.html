Median Meeple

January 2022 to Present

  • As a solo developer, I published Median Meeple to the Play Store in Q4 2022 and I actively maintain it.

  • In Median Meeple, a user can create a library of their favorite board games and record detailed data for each match that they play.

  • I use the Room persistence library to deliver a relational database consisting of five data models I created to represent game, match, and player data. Using this system, a user can create their own custom data model that reflects the scoring of each game to their preferred level of detail.

  • I leverage a MVVM architecture, using Jetpack Compose and Google's Material Design System 3 to present an intuitive and attractive UI to users. I also make use of Jetpack's Dagger/Hilt and the powerful Kotlin Coroutines.

RotMG DPS Calculator

May 2020 to August 2021

  • I published this app to the Play Store in Q1 2020, but the project ran out of steam in late 2021. It is no longer available on Play, as it is many years out of date.

  • I designed this app to assist a user playing the MMORPG, Realm of the Mad God. Using it, a player can calculate their damage-per-second or "DPS" with a certain set of gear, and then compare that result to another set of gear.

  • As there was no convenient API available at the time, I created and maintained my own JSON dataset containing the hundreds of equipment items obtainable in the game.

  • In the last update before I had to discontinue the project, I implemented support for showing the damage-per-second comparison on a line graph.
