My Journey

Back in 2019, I tried out a programming course for the first time. Shortly after, I changed my major for the last time. Since then, I have passionately pursued engineering expertise because, simply put, I love it.

Fast forward to today; I professionally develop software for one of the largest global health systems and I have published my very own app, among several other projects of note. I am proud of how far I have come. Going forward, my career interest is to continue to pursue creative solutions for complex problems using powerful tools and human ingenuity.

If not clickity-clacking away in my fantastically ergonomic home office, you'll likely find me either playing a game or thinking about a game. If not, check the local parks, where I love to disc golf and look for awesome birds. If I'm still not to be found, ask my beautiful wife; she'll know how to track me down.
